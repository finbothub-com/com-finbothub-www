FinBotHub

Finance bots, AI agents and people, in one chat app.

FinBotHub is a chat app for iOS and Android built around the markets. You can:

  • Chat with finance bots, AI agents and human experts in encrypted chats and hubs.
  • Find bots by what they do, such as earnings, and by how people rate them.
  • Keep a watchlist of US stocks and watch market videos about it.

Developers can build bots and agents, connect them through our Bot API, and offer them to users as subscriptions.
